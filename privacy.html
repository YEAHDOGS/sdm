This site

The future app

SDM will handle extremely sensitive data: sexual preferences, vice preferences, music taste, identity documents. The standing commitments:

Preference answers are opt-in, encrypted, and never shown on a public profile. Data is minimized, and real deletion means real deletion. Identity verification is handled by a third-party vendor; SDM will not warehouse government IDs.
