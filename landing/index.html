The plan

What we're building

Match by the music

Taste in sound says more than a bio ever will. Deep Spotify and Apple Music integration — profiles start with what you play.

Match by the rest of you

BDSM tests, personality, astrology, your vices of choice. Every axis feeds the match — only what you choose shows on the profile.

Verified animals only

Every account is backed by a valid US ID and a liveness check. 18+, humans only — AI keeps the bots and spam outside the fence.

Less swiping, more talking

A short daily batch of good matches instead of an infinite deck. Random text, voice, and video chat — and chatrooms for every scene.

Wild, not reckless

Privacy and consent tooling designed in from the first commit: encrypted preferences, per-field visibility, consent-gated media, real deletion.

No dark patterns

Premium buys capacity and control — never outcomes. No pay-per-match, no ransom on who liked you.

Under the hood

How it's built

The boring parts are boring on purpose, so the wild parts can be wild.

One codebase, every screen

iOS and Android first, web right behind — one TypeScript monorepo shared down to the pixel.

Serverless to the bone

The whole platform runs on edge compute that scales from zero — no server fleet, no 3 a.m. pager, nothing idling between matches.

Verified, not warehoused

ID checks happen at a specialist verification vendor. We keep a yes/no attestation — never your document, never your face.

Anonymous by default

Handles, age ranges, distance buckets. Nothing in the app links your SDM self to your legal one.
