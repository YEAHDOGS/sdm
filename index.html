01

Three signals. One animal.

i

Music

Match on the music you live to. Your listening builds a sonic fingerprint: a vibe score and one shared playlist, not a photo grid.

ii

Sex

Compatibility on your terms. Opt-in preference matching, explained out loud, never exposed. Your answers stay yours.

iii

Drugs

Your vices are a signal, not a transaction. Preferences feed matching. Buying or selling anything gets you banned.

02

How it moves

Less swiping, more talking.

Random text, voice, and video chat. Chatrooms anchored to shows, venues, and scenes. The jungle is the city after dark: concerts, afterparties, late-night diners. Find your pack.

Questions

Is sdm live?+

Not yet. This page is the stake in the ground while the app is being built. Follow the build on GitHub for progress.

What does it match on?+

Music first: your listening builds a sonic fingerprint. Then opt-in compatibility on sexual preferences and vices. Every match is explained, never exposed.

Is it really 18+?+

Strictly. ID verification gates every social feature. The name is rock and roll attitude; the age gate is not.

What about the drugs part?+

Preferences are a compatibility signal. Buying, selling, or facilitating anything is a ban. Full stop.

When and where?+

US only at launch. One beta city first, then the rest of the jungle. Timing will be announced here.
