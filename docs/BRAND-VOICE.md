# BRAND VOICE — sdm

**The rule:** sdm sounds like the afterparty, not the HR department. Every
other dating app copywrites like it's onboarding you at a bank; sdm
copywrites like it's texting you at 1 a.m. from the good show.

This guide keeps every surface — notifications, empty states, match cards,
errors, safety copy — consistent. Write to it, and the app always sounds
like the same animal.

## The jungle in five principles

1. **Confident, never desperate.** sdm assumes you're worth knowing.
   No "we miss you 🥺" — sdm pings you because something is actually
   happening.
2. **Music first, compliments second.** Lead with the shared song, not
   "you look great." The vibe score is the love language.
3. **Funny beats horny.** Edgy is attitude and nightlife, never explicit
   content. The product stays app-store-safe; the attitude stays
   after-hours.
4. **Short. Punchy. Text-speak energy.** Copy reads like a friend's
   text, not a press release. One breath per line.
5. **The pack metaphor does the heavy lifting.** Jungles, packs, hunts,
   shows, afterparties. Lean on it — but don't overstuff. One animal
   per sentence max.

## The voice in one line

> "Your pack is already at the show. You're late."

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Vibe score 87. Same 4 a.m. playlist energy." | "Our algorithm detected 87% compatibility." |
| "She's at the gig Friday too." | "A potential match will be at an event." |
| "Quiet night. No losses." | "You have 0 new matches. Keep trying!" |
| Report copy is dead serious, zero jokes. | Never joke in safety, age-gate, or report flows. |
| Swear-adjacent attitude, family-safe words. | No explicit sexual content anywhere. |

## Sample copy bank

### Push notifications

1. "Vibe score 91. They're literally going to the same show Friday. Run, don't walk."
2. "Someone in your pack just RSVP'd to Saturday's set. You're both going. This isn't a coincidence."
3. "Your sonic fingerprint says techno. The city says Friday. Coincidence? No."
4. "New match dropped: 88 vibe, same top artist. You two already agree on the important stuff."
5. "The pack is moving tonight and you haven't looked. Rude."
6. "He skipped your photo and matched on the playlist first. That's the point of this app."

### Empty states

7. "No matches yet. Either the jungle is quiet, or your taste is too elite. Add a gig and find out."
8. "Your deck is empty. The city isn't — widen the vibe radius or tell us what you're actually into."
9. "No messages. Go like somebody's taste. Worst case: great conversation."

### Match messages (the first line matters)

10. "Vibe score 87 — we both know every word of the third album. How are you single?"
11. "Same gig Friday, same top artist, zero excuses. What's the move?"
12. "Your 2 a.m. track is my 2 a.m. track. I'm scared how fast this is."

### Onboarding prompts (music before photos — always)

13. "Three artists that raised you. Go."
14. "Last gig you actually left the house for?"
15. "What are you listening to at 2 a.m. and don't lie."
16. "Pick your scene. No, really — the algorithm smells posers."

### Errors and edge cases

17. "The jungle glitched. Your matches are safe — we're shaking the tree. Try again in a sec."
18. "That's not a real age, chief. 18+ or the bouncer turns you around."
19. "Photo verification failed — try better light, face the camera. The pack needs to see you."

### Safety copy (dead serious, always)

Safety surfaces never carry the joke voice. They carry clarity.

20. "Blocked. They can't see you, message you, or match with you again — anywhere in the app."
21. "Thanks — our review team sees every report. If someone made you unsafe here, we take them out of the jungle."

## Notes for future copywriters

- Vibe score is always "vibe score", never "compatibility" or "match
  percentage". It's the brand's metric; name it like one.
- Nights/events are always *happening now* — copy lives in the
  present tense. "Friday" beats "upcoming".
- When in doubt, cut it in half. Then cut the emoji.
- App-store safety: attitude is in the wit, never in the content.
  Anything that could read as explicit, coercive, or substance-praising
  gets rewritten, no exceptions.
