# VOICE — sdm brand voice guide

The app's name is rock-and-roll attitude; the copy has to earn it without
ever tripping an app-store review. This is the reference for every surface:
push notifications, empty states, match messages, error copy, the landing
page.

**Core rule: sound like the afterparty, write like a compliance lawyer is
reading over your shoulder.** Edgy in energy, never edgy in content. If a
line would need a content warning, rewrite it.

## Tone principles

1. **Jungle framing, everywhere.** The city after dark is the jungle —
   venues are clearings, shows are watering holes, your matches are your
   pack. Use the metaphor as seasoning, not wallpaper.
2. **Confident, never thirsty.** sdm assumes you're already interesting.
   Never beg for engagement ("Come back! 😢"), never guilt-trip.
3. **Playful, never explicit.** "Sex, Drugs, Music" is a badge, not a
   content policy. Keep every line app-store-safe: no graphic content,
   no encouraging reckless behavior, no profanity-laced punchlines.
4. **Music-first vocabulary.** Reach for sonic language — frequency,
   signal, static, feedback, encore — before generic dating-app words.
5. **Anti-swipe energy.** Punch up at generic swipe apps occasionally;
   never punch down at users. Sarcastic toward the category, warm toward
   the pack.
6. **Short.** Push notifications under 12 words. Empty-state lines under
   two sentences. The afterparty doesn't monologue.

## Sample copy

### Push notifications

- "Your frequency is picking up. 3 new animals in your radius."
- "She was at the same show Friday. You were in the same room. Say hi."
- "Your vibe score with @mara just went gold. Worth a listen."
- "Gig radar: your pack is hitting Mohawk tonight. You in?"
- "Encore? You both unmatched your ex's taste. That's a start."

### Empty states

- **No matches yet:** "Quiet jungle. Answer a few more music questions
  and we'll tune your frequency."
- **No messages:** "The chat is silent because nobody's played the
  first track yet. Send one."
- **No upcoming gigs:** "Nothing on the radar. The wild is unpredictable
  — check back Thursday."

### Match messages (system-generated icebreakers)

- "You both ranked 'Loud Minor Blues' your #1 live track. Defend your
  honor."
- "Two techno heads in Tulsa. That's not a coincidence, that's a sign."
- "Your overlap playlist is 47 songs deep. Press play before you type."

### Onboarding microcopy

- "Music first. Photos later. We're not that kind of app."
- "Pick the three artists you'd defend in a bar fight."
- "Fresh ears? No history yet — your score starts provisional and gets
  sharper as you listen."

### Errors / edge cases

- "Static on the line — that didn't go through. Try again."
- "You're 17 steps from the venue but the gig already sold out. The
  pack forgives you."

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Find your pack" | "Find hot singles near you" |
| "Your frequency is picking up" | "We miss you! Come back 😢" |
| Reference shared music concretely | "You both like music 🎵" |
| Tease generic swipe apps | Mock other users or their taste |
| Keep it under 12 words in pushes | Paragraphs in notifications |

## Hard lines (never cross)

- No sexual content in copy — the name is the limit, not the license.
- No encouraging drinking, drugs, or reckless behavior, even as a joke.
- No profanity in user-facing system copy (user chat is moderated
  separately under the moderation policy in CONCEPT.md §5).
- No dark patterns: no fake urgency ("3 people want you RIGHT NOW"),
  no shame, no guilt.
- Accessibility: never rely on jungle metaphor alone — the meaning must
  be clear if the user doesn't get the bit.

## Using this guide

Any new screen or notification should trace back to one tone principle
above. If the brand voice blocks you from saying something clearly,
clarity wins — the voice is a default, not a straitjacket. When in
doubt, read it aloud in a bar after a show: if it sounds like a friend,
ship it.
