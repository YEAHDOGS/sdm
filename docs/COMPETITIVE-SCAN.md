## Takeaways for sdm

1. **The graveyard is density, not concept.** Tastebuds (oldest, biggest) and Vinylly (best tech: passive Spotify data) both die the same way: too few users per city → dead queues → churn. sdm's seed-city launch (TASKS.md #17) is the right counter — win ONE festival weekend before city #2.
2. **Passive data beats self-declared.** Vinylly's Spotify-history matching is the mechanic users praise; Tastebuds' manual artist entry is friction. sdm should do BOTH: quiz for onboarding virality (TASKS #5), verified streaming data as the trust layer (TASKS #12).
3. **Don't paywall the core loop.** Tastebuds gates messaging — the thing that creates couples — behind $10/mo and reviews complain. Gate *accelerants* instead (rewinds, see-your-admirers, gig alerts — already in TASKS #18).
4. **Concerts are the killer feature nobody monetized well.** Vinylly's in-chat "Suggest a Concert" is beloved and under-built. sdm's Gig Radar (TASKS #14) + same-show match boost is the sharpest wedge in this list — lean into it.
5. **Price anchor: $10/mo.** Tastebuds and the Vinylly Pro tier both land at $10/mo for full premium. sdm's Backstage Pass should launch at $9.99/mo to undercut-by-a-dollar the incumbents.
