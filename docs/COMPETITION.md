# COMPETITIVE SCAN — sdm

Five apps in the music-meets-dating lane, plus what each one teaches us.
Researched 2026-09-09. One page of notes, not a thesis.

## 1. Tastebuds (2010–2023) — the cautionary tale

- **What:** The original music-based dating site. Users entered
  favorite artists (or imported via Last.fm / Facebook / Spotify /
  iTunes), got matched by musical taste, and could message each other.
- **Features:** Artist-based matching, a proprietary match algorithm,
  "Gig Roulette" (generates a nearby concert + a person to go with),
  "Event Matchmaker" (find a date for a specific gig), Spotify app
  integration. Claimed 43% first-message response rate — higher than
  non-music dating sites at the time.
- **Pricing:** Started free; messaging paywalled for new web users from
  2014 (free on the iPhone app). Raised $600k seed (2012).
- **Reviews/legacy:** Well-covered by the Guardian and NME, ~100k+
  members across 100 countries at peak. Actual married couples and at
  least one "Tastebuds baby."
- **Where it failed:** Shut down **April 18, 2023** — "no longer viable
  to keep running." A dating app's fixed costs (moderation, infra,
  app-store compliance) outran revenue. No premium tier worth paying
  for, no network-density strategy.
- **Lesson for sdm:** Matching on music works — the engagement numbers
  were real. But free-core + no monetization + no seed-market density
  plan = death. This is why sdm's Backstage Pass premium and seed-city
  launch are P1–P4, not afterthoughts.

## 2. POM (Power of Music) — the hype that stalled

- **What:** UK app (2021) matching users on music history via Spotify /
  Apple Music, framed as "emotional compatibility," not just shared
  artists. Six personality questions on top of streaming data.
- **Features:** Streaming-data matching, emotional-profile algorithm,
  curated "POM Parties" offline events in London. Manual entry
  deliberately distrusted ("no hiding — you can't display songs that
  aren't in your library").
- **Pricing:** Free at launch; monetization never got real before
  traction did.
- **Reviews/legacy:** Big press (DJ Mag, Mixmag, Virgin Radio),
  £1.5M angel raise, 25,000-person launch waitlist — and then the
  August 2021 launch slipped with no new date. Buzz without a shipped
  product.
- **Where it failed:** Waitlist vanity metrics that never converted,
  launch delays, and an "emotional profile" black box nobody could
  explain or trust.
- **Lesson for sdm:** Ship first, market second. And keep the match
  score explainable — sdm's vibe score shows *why* ("same 4 a.m.
  playlist energy"), not "the algorithm feels something." POM's opacity
  was the product's weakest organ.

## 3. Vinylly — the direct rival

- **What:** Currently the largest music-first dating app. Spotify sync
  *or* a profile generator for non-streamers — no streaming account
  required to sign up.
- **Features:** Discover / Play / Chat / Date loop; concert search,
  suggestions, and ticket purchase inside chat ("Suggest a Concert"
  feature — stops chatting, starts dating); matches deepen as you
  stream more.
- **Pricing:** Free core with premium upgrades (typical freemium lane).
- **Reviews/legacy:** Alive and growing; strongest in major metro areas.
- **Where it falls short:** Generic swiping DNA wearing a vinyl costume.
  No scene-based discovery (no "techno heads in Tulsa"), no gig-anchored
  matching beyond ticket suggestions, no brand personality — it reads
  like any other dating app with a playlist feature bolted on.
- **Lesson for sdm:** This is the competitor to beat on *brand and
  scenes*. Vinylly owns "music dating" as a category; sdm wins by
  being the jungle (voice, scenes, gig-first discovery) instead of
  another swipe deck with a music tab.

## 4. FanMatch — the event-first niche

- **What:** Chicago-built app connecting singles going to the *same
  music festivals* — festivals selected from a list, chat free for
  matches.
- **Features:** Festival/event picker on the profile, mutual-interest
  matching per event, phone-verified "Real" status, moderator-approved
  photos before messaging.
- **Pricing:** Free core; unlimited messaging to non-matches at
  **$3.99/week, $9.99/month, $19.99/3 months**.
- **Reviews/legacy:** Small beta; never reached scale.
- **Where it failed:** Festivals-only is too narrow (a few weekends a
  year), density problem brutal, and the explicit/hookup-forward
  framing kept it off the app stores' good side and out of the press
  cycle that drives installs.
- **Lesson for sdm:** Event-anchored matching is validated as a *want*;
  the mistake was events-ONLY. sdm does gig radar as a feature inside
  a full dating app (seed city → weekly shows, not just festivals) and
  keeps the brand edgy but store-safe, per BRAND-VOICE.md rule #3.

## 5. Tinder Festival Mode — the incumbent's answer

- **What:** Tinder's Explore feature: add upcoming festivals
  (Bonnaroo, EDC, Lollapalooza, 20+ across 10 countries) to your
  profile and swipe people attending the same event. Built with Live
  Nation / AEG / Superstruct partnerships.
- **Features:** Festival badges, a "Festival Goers" general space,
  standard Tinder swipe/match/chat underneath.
- **Pricing:** Inside Tinder's existing subscription (Plus/Gold/
  Platinum).
- **Reviews/legacy:** Reached millions; Tinder reports 61% of users
  "became friends, or more" with someone met at a festival. Music is
  Tinder's #1 interest globally.
- **Where it falls short (for us):** A seasonal tab on a generic app.
  No music-taste matching at all — it's "going to the same festival,"
  not "same sonic fingerprint." Zero personality beyond the badge.
- **Lesson for sdm:** Validates the demand at massive scale (music is
  the #1 interest) while leaving the actual music-matching lane wide
  open. Nobody with real listening-data compatibility has won the
  category yet. That's the gap.

## The one-line takeaway

Tastebuds proved the demand and died of no monetization. POM proved
hype without shipping is worthless. Vinylly owns the category but has
no soul. FanMatch proved event-anchored matching works but only inside
a full app. Tinder proved music is the #1 interest and still hasn't
built music matching. sdm's opening: **explainable vibe score +
scene discovery + jungle voice + a premium tier from day one.**
